GOOD TO KNOW

  • Boulders fall when you dig beneath them. Never dig straight down under one - but do drop them on creatures.
  • Earth hides you. Most creatures hunt by sight, so every open tunnel is a way to be seen. Up close they sense you anyway.
  • Stars are for speed: three with 35 % of the clock left at the exit, two with 15 %.
  • Reading is free. The first time you find an element the clock holds for a few seconds (📖).
  • Finds are kept. Every element you find stays in your field notebook, even if the mine gets you - until you close the game.

CREATURES

Cave batFlies, and hunts by sight; within a few tiles it senses you through earth too. Get clear and put earth between you. Boulders, floods and gas kill it.
Mine snakeIgnores you until you stray close - three tiles in easy mines, up to six in hard ones - then strikes fast and holds on while you are near. Swims. Crush it with a boulder.
Cave spiderBlind, but feels digging up to 15 tiles away and a pick-up within 5; in harder mines it also notices you a tile or two off. Walk, don’t dig. Dynamite its nest before the brood grows.
Crystal crabSlow, and digs through earth to reach you. Falling boulders bounce off its shell - use dynamite, or simply outwalk it.

TOOLS

1 · DynamitePlace it and run. The fuse is short: every step you take and every second that passes burns it, five in all. Clears earth, boulders and ice, but not solid rock. The noise draws creatures.
2 · Helmet lampDoubles how far you can see for 12 seconds.
3 · Geiger counterReveals hidden bonus elements for 15 seconds. Dig them while it runs.
4 · Gas maskTwenty seconds of safe breathing in gas.
5 · Water pumpStand beside a flood to place it. Drains the water and caps the spring.
KeysEach key opens one locked door. Answer pads open the glowing logic doors: step on the right element. A wrong pad costs 30 seconds, and the answers change places every time.

READING AN ELEMENT CARD

🏷️
Supply riskHow easily the world’s supply could be disrupted. Low: many producers. Medium: fairly concentrated. High: a few countries control it and it is hard to replace. Critical: the highest level - one country or one by-product stream supplies most of it, and nothing replaces it easily.
⚖️
Reality checkHow the element is really obtained. Nobody picks up glowing crystals: most come from ore, brine, air or as a byproduct of something else.
🔧
In this deviceOn a mission, each card says what that element actually does in the device you are building.

Underlined wordsPoint at or tap a dotted-underlined word, such as cathode or alloy, to see what it means.

HAZARDS & MACHINES

BouldersPush them sideways; they fall when unsupported. One landing on you ends the dig.
WaterFloods downhill from hidden springs. You wade at half speed; snakes do not slow down.
LavaOne step in is fatal. Dig into a glowing vein and the lava breaks out in front of you: stop there, and go round. It then creeps outward and down, never up.
Mine gasRises and spreads from broken pipes. Suffocates in seconds - and explodes if any blast touches it.
Toxic barrelsExplode when they fall or are struck. Push one into a pit and stand well back.
Ice and basaltYour pickaxe will not mark ice: it takes a blast. Dark basalt starts to crack once you see it and crumbles away within seconds: mind whatever it holds up.
MinecartsShove one to send it rolling over anything on the line, or step down onto it to ride.
Runaway carts and liftsOld machines that never stop. Touching one is fatal: watch the rhythm, then cross.
🚧
Tunnel borerGrinds through earth, rock and ice in its lane and turns back at a wall. Never stand in front; follow the tunnel it leaves behind.
Refuge chambersStep inside to save your progress. If the mine claims you, you can return there for 100 points.
